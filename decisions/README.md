# Decisions

Numbered records of decisions about the basemap: what it is built from, how it is drawn, and how it is served. Each record says what was decided, why, and what was rejected. Superseded records are marked, not deleted.

The decisions that started this project were made in SalishSea.io and stay there:

- [068](https://github.com/salish-sea/salishsea-io/blob/main/docs/decisions/068-the-map-stays-on-openlayers.md): SalishSea.io builds its own basemap. (Its choice of OpenLayers is superseded by [076](https://github.com/salish-sea/salishsea-io/blob/main/docs/decisions/076-the-map-moves-to-maplibre.md): the map is drawn by MapLibre GL.)
- [073](https://github.com/salish-sea/salishsea-io/blob/main/docs/decisions/073-the-basemap-depths-are-30-m-lightly-shaded.md): one 30 m depth grid for the whole range, shaded lightly. (Amended by 001 and 002 below.)
- [074](https://github.com/salish-sea/salishsea-io/blob/main/docs/decisions/074-the-basemap-is-its-own-project-and-the-cards-draw-on-it.md): the basemap is this repository, served from tiles.salishsea.io as vector and image tiles.

Recorded here:

- [001](001-canadas-depth-grid-comes-first.md): Canada's depth grid is used wherever it has data, NOAA's elsewhere, each kept in its own vertical datum.
- [002](002-the-relief-is-shaded-by-maplibre.md): the relief is shaded by MapLibre from elevation tiles, not baked into image tiles. (Amended 2026-10-21: measured on a Galaxy S22, it costs about half a second on first render and nothing in frame rate.)
- [003](003-land-is-flat-except-parks.md): land is one flat colour, shaped by the relief, except for parks in a faint green; rivers and streams are a clear blue.
- [004](004-ground-that-dries-at-low-tide-is-drawn.md): ground that dries at low tide is drawn from the depth grid, in khaki.
- [005](005-ferry-routes-are-drawn-from-zoom-9.md): ferry routes are drawn from zoom 9, one line per channel up to zoom 12, and named from zoom 11.
- [006](006-marine-names-and-the-boundary-come-from-government-gazetteers.md): marine names come from GNIS and Canada's names database, placed and timed against the depth grid, and replace Protomaps' names for bays and straits; the boundary at sea is NOAA's.
- [007](007-published-builds-are-dated-and-never-change.md): each build is published once, under a CalVer path on tiles.salishsea.io such as `2026.10.1`, tagged in git, and never changed; versions share archives, each stored once under a name made from its contents.
- [008](008-full-builds-run-on-a-fly-machine.md): full builds run on a temporary Fly machine with a volume for the source grids, and publish from there. (Superseded by 021.)
- [009](009-gebco-fills-the-deep-ocean.md): GEBCO's global grid fills the deep ocean, and every grid is blended in at its edges.
- [010](010-fonts-and-icons-are-served-with-the-tiles.md): the labels' fonts and the icons are copied from Protomaps' assets at one commit and served from tiles.salishsea.io, under a path that never changes.
- [011](011-the-coast-covers-salishsea-ios-data.md): the coast region covers SalishSea.io's killer whale data, 136–120°W, 36–54.8°N.
- [012](012-a-coarse-map-covers-the-northeast-pacific.md): a coarse map from GEBCO covers 180–90°W, 15–70°N, so maps can zoom out to 3 and pan past the region without anything blank.
- [013](013-the-tiles-are-in-web-mercator.md): every tile is in Web Mercator, because MapLibre draws no other grid and PMTiles can't name another; UTM zone 10N was rejected, as it fits only 126–120°W.
- [014](014-image-tiles-are-drawn-from-the-style-along-the-shore.md): image tiles, for pictures stitched from tiles, are drawn from the style without its labels, by MapLibre GL in headless Chromium; zooms 11 to 15 only along the Salish Sea's shore.
- [015](015-landmarks-are-what-the-charts-mark-and-tall-towers.md): landmarks are named lighthouses and major lights, what the charts mark as conspicuous, and radio and television towers that are charted or 100 m tall, from OpenStreetMap; points within 500 m are one landmark.
- [016](016-points-of-interest-are-where-people-watch-from.md): of Protomaps' points of interest, only beaches, parks and nature reserves (drawn as parks), peaks, marinas, ferry terminals, aerodromes, museums, zoos, and at the closest zooms benches, toilets and drinking water; shops, restaurants and other businesses are left out.
- [017](017-depth-labels-are-set-in-a-serif.md): depth labels are set in Noto Serif, turned along their lines, because Noto Sans's 1 turned along a climbing line reads as a 7; the serif is served with Protomaps' fonts under `assets/fonts-<hash>/`.
- [018](018-the-names-the-map-labels-are-published-as-lists.md): the Salish Sea's places a search offers, about 2,100 of the names the map labels (the gazetteers' larger water bodies and points; OpenStreetMap's larger islands, cities, towns and villages, and one ferry terminal for each landing), are published with each version as two JSON lists, apart because their licences differ, and the style names them in its metadata as `salishsea:places`.
- [019](019-shipping-lanes-are-drawn-from-the-charts.md): shipping lanes, two-way routes and precautionary areas are drawn from zoom 8 as faint raspberry outlines, with arrows from 10 and names from 12, the separation zones undrawn, from NOAA's charts wherever NOAA charts and Canada's where it doesn't; on the map, not in the image tiles.
- [020](020-each-river-mouth-is-labelled-with-its-salmon-runs.md): each named river system that reaches the sea is labelled where it does with its salmon and steelhead runs, such as "Chinook (spring, summer, fall) · coho · chum (fall)", from WDFW and NWIFC's SWIFD (systems built from its lines) and Fisheries and Oceans Canada's NuSEDS (by watershed code); placed before towns' names; swept for labels that come and go before a release; not in the image tiles.
- [021](021-full-builds-run-on-hostess.md): full builds run in Docker on hostess, Peter's desktop, with the source grids kept in `~/basemap-data`, and publish from there; the Fly machine of 008 is retired.
- [022](022-the-protomaps-build-is-moved-forward-before-each-version.md): the Protomaps build stays pinned, and is moved to the newest daily build before each version is built for publishing, because Protomaps keeps only about a week of them.
- [023](023-peninsulas-are-named-from-a-zoom-set-by-their-size.md): peninsulas are named from OpenStreetMap's outlines, in the islands' grey italic, from two zooms before each fits one tile: the Olympic and Kitsap Peninsulas from zoom 6, Key Peninsula from 7; Protomaps' own peninsula points, all from zoom 12, are left undrawn.
- [024](024-natural-features-are-named-before-towns-and-cities-before-them.md): where labels collide, cities' names win, then straits' and sounds', then islands' and peninsulas', then towns', landmarks', points of interest' and roads'; so Hope Island is named before Hope Island Marine State Park, and Vancouver before Burrard Peninsula.
- [025](025-a-planetiler-profile-may-replace-our-openstreetmap-overrides.md) (proposed, not decided): building the Protomaps tiles ourselves from a fork of its Planetiler profile, with our islets', peninsulas' and parks' rules in it, in place of layers that correct it; revisit when another override is wanted.
- [026](026-parks-are-named-at-their-headquarters-and-reserves-tinted-on-the-land.md): the names of parks at least 1 km across are moved from where Protomaps puts them to the park's headquarters, office, ranger station or visitor centre, if it has one; nature reserves named as parks, as many state parks are, are tinted as parks, from outlines trimmed to the land.
- [027](027-every-water-name-has-a-region-and-the-regions-cover-the-water.md): every water name the map labels has one region, which holds its label, and the regions cover the water (with the flats, less OpenStreetMap's land), from 42°N to 51.5°N; outlined names take their outlines, the smallest winning, others the water their shore encloses or along it, and the rest of the water joins the nearest through the water; repeated names are told apart by island, town or larger water; from government data only, not OpenStreetMap, in `regions.pmtiles`, hidden, and `places-regions.json`.
